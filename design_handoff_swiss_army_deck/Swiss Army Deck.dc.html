Mapping strategy

Every identity is derived from the playing card, so nothing has to be memorised twice. Deck A carries the 52 minor arcana less the knights; the four knights sit on deck B's jacks. The 22 majors take deck B's spades in rank order, then deck B's hearts ace through ten.

Arboretum species are the eight suit-and-deck pairs, valued by rank ace through eight; the ninth and tenth species are the eight nines and the eight tens, valued in the same suit-deck order. Courts carry no species, which is why the centre field is free for the majors and courts to use.

Bohnanza categories ignore the deck letter entirely: both copies of a card share a category, so the eight bands are contiguous runs of the deck read spades to clubs, ace to king — ten pairs, then nine, eight, seven, six, five, four and three.

Reading the face
CornersRank and suit, diagonally mirrored. Red suits are set in primary, not scarlet. HeadDeck letter with a gold rhomb for B, ink for A. Left railTarot, rotated out of the reading path until wanted.
