Tarot

Deck A is the minor arcana entire, less the knights: ace through ten, then page, queen, king, in swords, cups, pentacles and wands. The four knights are deck B's jacks.

The 22 majors run through deck B's spades in rank order — ace to ten, then queen and king for the wheel and justice — and continue on deck B's hearts, ace through ten, for the hanged man to the world.

Twenty-six cards carry no tarot identity: deck B's diamonds and clubs apart from the jacks, and deck B's queen and king of hearts.

Face

Corners: rank and suit. Head: deck letter, gold rhomb for B. Left rail: tarot. Centre: Arboretum species and value, or the tarot card where there is no species. Foot: Bohnanza category under a gold hairline.
