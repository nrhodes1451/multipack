3c Card back
The deck's own marks: the eight flowers in paper checkered with the eight cut stones in primary-tint, filling a primary panel inside a gold hairline frame. No text, and every mark upright.
Arboretum · eight flowers, values ace–king
{{s.name}} {{s.rule}}
One flower per suit × deck, so every card has a species; the value beside it is the rank, ace through king. Deck A flowers are set in ink, deck B in primary, and no two silhouettes are alike — the name never appears on the card.
Stones · Bohnanza distribution
{{b.name}} {{b.rule}}
Quartz is the commonest at twenty and diamond the scarcest at six, exactly Bohnanza's ladder. Stones ignore the deck letter, so both copies of a card carry the same one and the eight runs are contiguous, spades to clubs, ace to king.
